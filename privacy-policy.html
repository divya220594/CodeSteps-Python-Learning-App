CodeSteps Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

CodeSteps is a beginner Python learning app. This policy applies to the Android app, CodeSteps: Python Basics.

Information the app handles

CodeSteps does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information. Lesson progress is stored locally on your device using Android app storage so you can continue learning. This information is not sent to the developer.

Permissions, ads, and analytics

This version does not request sensitive device permissions, show advertisements, use analytics, or process payments.

Data retention and deletion

Local lesson progress remains on your device until you reset progress, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. The developer does not receive or retain this progress.

Children

This first version is designed as a general beginner learning tool and is not specifically directed to children. It does not collect personal information from users.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, analytics, payments, or other data handling, this policy and the Play Store disclosures must be updated before release.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact details displayed on the CodeSteps Google Play listing.

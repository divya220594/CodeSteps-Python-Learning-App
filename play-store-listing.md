# Draft Google Play listing

**App name:** CodeSteps: Python Basics

**Short description:** Start learning Python with friendly, bite-sized lessons in English or Tamil.

**Full description:**

New to coding? CodeSteps helps you take your first steps in Python through short explanations, examples, and quick quizzes.

- Learn the basics: print statements, variables, if statements, and loops.
- Switch between English and Tamil.
- Practice with short multiple-choice checks and explanations.
- Save lesson progress on your device.
- Learn offline, with no account required.

CodeSteps is an independent learning app for beginners. It does not provide formal qualifications or replace a course or instructor.

**Initial release:** 4 beginner lessons; English and Tamil text; progress stored locally; no ads, analytics, account, or in-app payments.
